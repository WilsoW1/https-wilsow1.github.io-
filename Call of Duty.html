CALL OF DUTY: MODERN WARFARE

My Favourite Video Game

About the Game

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare is one of my favourite video games. It was made by Infinity Ward and released in 2019. It is a first-person shooter game. The game has a story mode and multiplayer modes. Players can fight enemies, complete missions, and work as a team.

Why I Like It

Firstly, the gameplay is fast and exciting. I can use different weapons and play on different maps. In addition, I enjoy playing with a team. The gunshots, explosions, and footsteps make the game feel more real.

I first learned about this game from my friends. Then, I started playing it and became interested in it. My favourite part is multiplayer because every game can be different. I would recommend it to people who like action, teamwork, and competition.

Colours and Design

The game often uses black, dark green, grey, and brown. Black and grey make the game look serious. In addition, dark green reminds me of the military. These colours match the military theme of the game.

Fonts

The game uses simple and bold fonts. The text looks strong and modern. In addition, it is easy to read. This style works well with the military theme.

Final Thoughts

Overall, I like Modern Warfare because it is exciting and fun. The game is not only about shooting. Players also need to think, listen, and work as a team. These things keep me interested in the game.
